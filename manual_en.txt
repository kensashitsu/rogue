1. Introduction
================================================================================

  Rogue is the original dungeon-exploring game, born in the 1980s.
  It is turn-based: time stands still until you act.
  The map is randomly generated, so every dungeon is different.
  When you die, your save is deleted (permadeath). Play carefully.

  [Display Language]
    You can switch between English and Japanese: press Tab on the name
    entry screen, or the "o" key during play.
    The chosen language is saved in rogue.cfg and is used again next time.
    If Japanese text looks unevenly spaced, set the Windows Terminal font to
    a monospace font that includes Japanese, such as "BIZ UDGothic".

================================================================================
2. Goal of the Game
================================================================================

  Your goal is to get the Amulet of Yendor, hidden on floor 26,
  and return alive to the surface.

  If you try to leave for the surface (above floor 1) without the Amulet,
  you will fail.
  Once you have the Amulet, climb the stairs with the "<" command and head
  for the surface from floor 1.

  [Starting Equipment]
    You begin the game with the following:
      - a +1,+1 mace (wielded)
      - a +1,+0 short bow (in your pack)
      - 25-39 arrows (in your pack)
      - +1 ring mail, AC 6 (worn)
      - 1 ration of food (in your pack)

================================================================================

  The screen is 80x24 characters.

  │ ┌───────────────────────┐                                                  │
  │ │ ─────────────── . . . │                                                  │
  │ │ │       @      │ # . .│  <- dungeon map (rows 1-22)                      │
  │ │ │              │ #    │                                                  │
  │ │ └──────────────┘      │                                                  │

  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  [Map Symbols]
    @    the player (you)
    .    room floor
    #    corridor
    +    door
    ─ │  wall
    %    stairs (> to go down / < to go up)
    ^    trap
    *    gold
    !    potion
    ?    scroll
    :    food
    )    weapon
    ]    armor
    =    ring
    /    wand or staff
    ,    the Amulet of Yendor
    A-Z  monsters

  [Status Line]
    Level:X    current dungeon floor (floor number)
    Gold:X     gold pieces carried
    Hp:X(X)    current HP (max HP)
    Str:X(X)   current strength (max strength)
    Arm:X      effective armor class (lower is tougher)
    Exp:X/X    player level / total experience points

  Hunger and status effects are shown as short words at the end of the
  status line.
    Hungry / Weak / Faint     hunger (see "7. Hunger and Food")
    Conf / Blind / Para etc.  status effects (see "11. Status Effects")

  When there are more messages, "--More--" appears at the end.
  Press the space bar to go to the next message.

4. Basic Controls
================================================================================

  [Moving]
    vi keys (the standard roguelike keys):
      y  k  u
      h  @  l
      b  j  n

      k = north       j = south       h = west        l = east
      y = northwest   u = northeast   b = southwest   n = southeast

    Arrow keys:
      Up / Down / Left / Right = north / south / west / east

    Numeric keypad (NumLock on):
      7 8 9
      4 5 6   (5 = wait)
      1 2 3

    Numeric keypad (NumLock off):
      Home/PgUp/End/PgDn = diagonal moves

  [Running (capital letters)]
    H J K L Y U B N = keep running until you hit a wall, door or monster

  [When a Monster Is in the Way]
    If a monster is in the direction you move, you attack it automatically.

5. Command List
================================================================================

  [Moving and Actions]
    h j k l y u b n   move in 8 directions (vi keys)
    H J K L Y U B N   run in 8 directions
    arrow keys        move in 4 directions
    numeric keypad    move in 8 directions (5 = wait)
    .                 rest for 1 turn
    Z                 rest to heal (keep resting until HP is full and
                      blindness, hallucination, confusion, etc. are cured;
                      stops if anything happens)
    s                 search adjacent squares (find hidden doors and traps)
    f                 fight a monster in the given direction
    >                 go down the stairs (you must be standing on %)
    <                 go up the stairs (you must be standing on %)

  [Items]
    i    open your inventory and use an item
    d    drop an item on the floor
    e    eat food (: items)
    r    read a scroll (? items)
    q    quaff (drink) a potion (! items)
    w    wield a weapon () items)
    W    wear armor (] items)
    T    take off armor or put away your weapon
    P    put on a ring (= items)
    R    remove a ring
    t    throw an item (choose a direction)
    z    zap a wand or staff (/ items; choose a direction)

  [Information and System]
    ?    show help (on a Japanese keyboard, F1 also works)
    F1   show help (same as ?)
    @    show your character's status in detail
    D    list the items whose identity you have found out
    /    look up what a map symbol means
    ^    check the trap you are standing on
    Ctrl-P   show the last message again
    Ctrl-R   redraw the screen
    o    switch the display language (English / Japanese; it is kept for
         next time)
    S    save and quit
    Q    give up and quit (your score is recorded, counted as a death)

  * Wielding/wearing (w/W/P), removing (T/R), throwing (t) and zapping (z)
    each take 1 turn.
  * With "i" you can open your inventory and use items directly.
    The commands for each kind (e/r/q/w/W, etc.) list only items of that
    kind.

6. Stats
================================================================================

  [Hp (Hit Points)]
    At 0 you die. When you are not fighting, HP slowly comes back over time.
      Lv1-7:  after a while without fighting, recover 1 at a time
              (about 1 per 19 turns at Lv1, about 1 per 7 turns at Lv7)
      Lv8+:   after 3 turns without fighting, recover 1 to (Lv-7) every turn
    Attacking or being attacked resets the wait for recovery.
    Each ring of regeneration adds +1 per turn.

  [Str (Strength)]
    Affects your bonuses to hit and to damage.
    It has two values: current (outside the parentheses) and max (inside).
    Poison and Rattlesnake bites lower the current value for a while
    (the max does not drop).
    A potion of gain strength raises the max by +1.
    A potion of restore strength brings the current value back to the max.

    STR and hit/damage bonuses, roughly (max STR: 31):
      STR  3- 5:  hit -3 to -1   / damage -3 to -1
      STR  6-13:  no bonus
      STR 14-15:  hit +1         / damage +0 to +1
      STR 16-18:  hit +2         / damage +1 to +2
      STR 19-21:  hit +2 to +3   / damage +2 to +3
      STR 25-26:  hit +6 to +7   / damage +5 to +6
      STR 27-31:  hit +7 to +10  / damage +7 to +10

  [Arm (AC, Armor Class)]
    The lower it is, the better your defense. It starts at 10 (no armor).
    Wearing armor lowers it.

    Base AC of each armor:
      leather armor     8
      ring mail         7
      studded leather   7
      scale mail        6
      chain mail        5
      splint mail       4
      banded mail       4
      plate mail        3

  [Player Level and Experience]
    Killing monsters earns experience. At certain totals you level up.
    On a level up, max HP rises by a random 3-12 (the original's 1-10,
    plus 2). The highest level is 20.

    Experience needed (total):
      Lv2:10  Lv3:20  Lv4:40  Lv5:80   Lv6:160   Lv7:320   Lv8:640
      Lv9:1300  Lv10:2600  Lv11:5200  Lv12:13000  Lv13:26000  Lv14:50000
      Lv15:100000  Lv16:200000  Lv17:400000  Lv18:800000  Lv19:2000000
      Lv20:4000000

7. Hunger and Food
================================================================================

  You use up food every turn.
  The rate is "base 1 + extra for each ring you wear". While you carry the
  Amulet, the base becomes 0.
    1/turn:         protection, add strength, sustain strength, stealth,
                    maintain armor
    2/turn:         regeneration
    sometimes 1:    searching, dexterity, increase damage (1/3),
                    see invisible (1/5)
    0:              adornment, aggravate monster, teleportation
    slow digestion: 1 in 2 chance of lowering the use by 1

  [Hunger Display]
    When you get hungry, the status line and a message tell you.

    Shown   Message                             Effect
    ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    Hungry  "You are starting to get hungry."   none (under 300 left)
    Weak    "You are starting to feel weak."    none (under 150 left)
    Faint   "You feel too weak from lack of     when food runs out you
            food.  You faint."                  sometimes faint and can't
                                                move for a few turns.
                                                850 more turns without
                                                eating and you starve

  [How to Eat]
    Use the "e" command, or open the inventory with "i" and choose food (:).
    You can't eat when you are full (max capacity: 2000).

  [Kinds of Food]
    ration of food      Ordinary food. 70% of the time it tastes awful,
                        30% of the time it tastes good.
                        Either way it fills you up enough.
    slime-mold          A kind of food. Always tastes good, and gives
                        +1 experience.

8. Combat
================================================================================

  [Your Attacks]
    If a monster is in the direction you move, you attack automatically.
    Hit roll: d20 + player level + hit bonus >= 21 - monster AC
    Damage: your weapon's dice + damage bonus (bare hands: 1d4)

  [Weapon Damage] (melee damage / thrown damage)
    mace              2d4 / 1d3    long sword        3d4 / 1d2
    short bow         1d1 / 1d1    arrow             1d1 / 2d3
    dagger            1d6 / 1d4    two handed sword  4d4 / 1d2
    dart              1d1 / 1d3    shuriken          1d2 / 2d4
    spear             2d3 / 1d6

  [Weapons and Tight Spaces]
    In narrow places such as corridors, doorways and mazes, long blades
    catch on the walls and hit less often.
      two handed sword: hit -4
      long sword:       hit -2
      other weapons (thrusting or short): no change
    A two handed sword takes both hands, so you can't parry: while you wield
    it, your defense (AC) is 1 worse.
    In rooms the two handed sword is the strongest, but in corridors a long
    sword or a mace hits more often.

  [Reading Weapon Names]
    "a +1,+2 long sword" -> a long sword with +1 to hit and +2 to damage
    "a +0,+0 mace"       -> an ordinary, unenchanted mace (its base damage
                            of 2d4 is unchanged)

  [Monster Attacks]
    Hit roll: d20 + monster level >= 21 - your effective AC

  [Throwing]
    Throw items with the "t" command.
    If you throw arrows while wielding a short bow, the bow's bonuses are
    added to hit and damage.
    Arrows that hit are destroyed; only arrows that miss fall where they land
    and can be picked up.

  [Saving Throws]
    When hit by a special attack, you avoid the effect if
    d20 + save value >= 14 + difficulty.
    The higher your player level, the easier it is.
    Wearing a ring of protection adds a bonus to your save value.

9. Items
================================================================================

  Potions (!), scrolls (?), rings (=) and wands/staffs (/) are found
  with an unknown appearance (color, title, gem, material).
  Using one reveals what it is. The "D" command lists the ones you have
  found out.

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [Potions (!)]  quaff with the "q" command

    Name                   Effect
    ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    confusion              You become confused (harmful; lasts 5 turns)
    hallucination          You hallucinate (monsters and items look like
                           other characters; lasts about 50 turns)
    poison                 Lowers your current STR (harmful)
    gain strength          Max STR +1 (current STR rises by the same amount)
    see invisible          Lets you see invisible monsters for some turns
    healing                Restores HP (also cures blindness)
    extra healing          Restores a lot of HP (also cures confusion and
                           blindness)
    haste self             Speeds you up for a while (2 actions per turn)
    restore strength       Restores current STR to the max
    raise level            Raises your level at once
    monster detection      Senses the monsters on this floor (shown over the
                           map)
    magic detection        Senses where the items on this floor are (shown
                           over the map)
    blindness              You become blind (harmful; lasts about 50 turns)
    levitation             You float (you no longer step on traps)

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [Scrolls (?)]  read with the "r" command

    Name                   Effect
    ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    monster confusion      Your next attack confuses the monster
    magic mapping          Reveals the map of the whole floor
    hold monster           Stops monsters chasing you within 2 squares
                           (they don't move until you attack)
    sleep                  You fall asleep for a while (harmful)
    enchant armor          Improves your armor's AC by 1 and removes its
                           curse
    enchant weapon         Improves your weapon's hit or damage by +1
    identify               Reveals what any 1 item in your pack is
                           (works on potions, scrolls, weapons, armor,
                           rings, wands and staffs)
                           * You can cancel with ESC. The scroll is then
                             not used up.
    scare monster          While it lies on the floor, monsters won't come
                           near
                           * Reading it only makes you hear laughter
                             (reading wastes it)
                           * Once you have picked it up and dropped it, it
                             crumbles when you touch it
    food detection         Senses where the food on this floor is
    teleportation          Teleports you to a random place
    create monster         Summons a monster nearby (harmful)
    remove curse           Removes all curses from your equipment
    protect armor          Permanently protects the armor you are wearing
                           (no effect from Aquator corrosion or rust traps)
    aggravate monsters     Makes all monsters hostile (harmful)

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [Weapons ())]  wield with the "w" command / put away with "T"

    You can wield 1 weapon at a time.
    A cursed weapon can't be put away (you need a scroll of remove curse).
    You can also throw weapons with the "t" command.

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [Armor (])]  wear with the "W" command / take off with "T"

    You can wear 1 suit of armor at a time.
    The number in brackets is its actual AC (lower means better defense).
    Cursed armor can't be taken off (you need a scroll of remove curse).

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [Rings (=)]  put on with the "P" command / remove with "R"

    You can wear up to 2 rings, one on each hand.
    A cursed ring can't be removed (you need a scroll of remove curse).
    Each kind of ring raises your food use (see "7. Hunger and Food").

    Name                   Effect
    ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    protection             Improves AC (by its enchantment); also a bonus
                           to saving throws
    add strength           Raises STR (by its enchantment)
    sustain strength       Protects current STR (prevents loss from
                           Rattlesnakes and poison)
    searching              Searches automatically every turn (finds hidden
                           doors on its own)
    see invisible          You always see invisible monsters
    dexterity              Raises your hit bonus
    increase damage        Raises your damage bonus
    regeneration           +1 HP every turn (on top of natural recovery).
                           Uses a lot of food
    slow digestion         1 in 2 chance of lowering food use by 1
    stealth                Mean (M) monsters in your room no longer wake up
    adornment              An ornament that does nothing
    aggravate monster      All monsters wake up and chase you (always
                           cursed)
    teleportation          Teleports you at random once every 33 turns
                           (always cursed)
    maintain armor         Prevents rust and corrosion of your armor

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [Wands and Staffs (/)]  use with the "z" command (choose a direction)

    Each use lowers the charges by 1. Once identified, the charges are shown
    as "[X charges]".
    There are wands (metal) and staffs (wood); which kind is which changes
    from game to game.

    Name                   Effect
    ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    light                  Lights up the current room
    invisibility           Makes a monster invisible (a bad one)
    lightning              Fires a bolt of lightning
    fire                   Fires a bolt of fire
    cold                   Fires a bolt of cold
    magic missile          Fires a magic missile
    polymorph              Turns a monster into another monster
    haste monster          Speeds a monster up (don't use it)
    slow monster           Slows a monster down
    drain life             You lose HP//2, and each monster on this floor
                           takes that much damage
                           * With no monsters around, you lose no HP and
                             it is not identified
                           * Can't be used when your HP is 1 or less
    teleport away          Teleports a monster to a random place
    teleport to            Pulls a monster next to you
    cancellation           Cancels a monster's special abilities
    nothing                Does nothing (a bad one)

  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  [Food (:)]  eat with the "e" command
    Two kinds: ration of food and slime-mold. The slime-mold takes the name of
    the "favorite fruit" you are asked for after your name (empty = the last
    one; slime-mold the first time).

  [Gold (*)]  picked up automatically when you step on it (takes no
              inventory slot; no limit)

  [Traps (^)]
    Traps are hidden and look like the floor. The moment you step on one,
    the ^ appears and the trap goes off.
    The "s" command finds traps on adjacent squares (without setting them
    off).
    The "^" command shows what kind of trap you are standing on.
    While levitating you avoid all traps.

    Name                   Effect
    ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    trap door              You fall to the next floor down
    arrow trap             An arrow shoots at you (hit roll; 1d6 damage)
                           * An arrow that misses falls to the floor and
                             can be picked up
    sleeping gas trap      Paralyzed (can't act) for 5-9 turns
    bear trap              Paralyzed (can't act) for 3-5 turns
    teleport trap          Sends you to a random place on this floor
    dart trap              1d4 damage; if you fail a VS_POISON save,
                           current STR -1
                           A ring of sustain strength prevents the STR loss
    rust trap              Your worn armor's AC gets 1 worse
                           * Leather armor doesn't rust (immune)
                           * No effect on armor protected by a scroll of
                             protect armor, or with a ring of maintain armor
    mystery trap           Something odd seems to happen, but it does no
                           harm

  [The Amulet of Yendor (,)]
    The goal of the game; there is one on floor 26. Carry it in your pack.

  [How Monsters Are Chosen]
    The index is d = floor + rnd(0-9) - 6.
    If d < 0 it becomes rnd(0-4). If d > 25 it becomes rnd(0-4) + 21.
    The chance of any one index being chosen is about 10%.
    "Floors" is a rough range of floors where d can land on that index.

  [Waking Up]
    Mean(M) monsters:  when you enter their room, 2 in 3 chance to wake up
                       and start chasing you.
                       They don't wake up while you wear a ring of stealth.
    All monsters:      always wake up when you are next to them.
    While you are levitating, Mean(M) monsters don't react.

11. Status Effects
================================================================================

  Status effects are shown as short words at the right of the status line.

  Shown   Effect and cure
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Conf    The direction of moves and item use becomes random.
          Cured by time or a potion of healing.
  Blind   You can't see the map; you only notice adjacent squares.
          Cured by time or a potion of healing or extra healing.
  Para    You can't do anything (pressing keys only passes turns).
          Wears off with time. Watch out for Ice Monsters, scrolls of
          sleep and fainting.
  Halu    Monsters and items are shown as random characters.
          Wears off with time.
  Pois    Current STR drops by 1 every turn. Cured by time or a potion of
          restore strength.
          * Poison stops lowering STR once STR is 3 or less
  Haste   You can act twice per turn (food and status effects only count
          1 turn).
          Wears off by itself with time.
  Slow    You can act only once every 2 turns (monsters move twice).
          Wears off by itself with time.
  Lev     You pass over all traps.
          ISMEAN monsters are less likely to react.
          Caused by a potion of levitation. Wears off with time.
  Inv     You are invisible (monsters can't follow you unless they have
          CANSEE).
  Held    You are caught by a Venus Flytrap. You can't move, but you can
          attack.
          Freed when you kill the Flytrap or get away from it.

12. Saving and Score
================================================================================

  [Saving]
    The "S" command asks for confirmation, then saves and quits.
    The next time you start, the save is loaded automatically (the title
    screen is skipped).
    When you die, the save is deleted (permadeath).
    Closing the window also saves automatically.

  [Giving Up (Q)]
    The "Q" command asks for confirmation, then lets you give up the game.
    Nothing is saved; your score is recorded and the game ends (counted as
    a death).

  [Score]
    Score = gold carried + total experience
    (If you win, player level x 100 is added.)

  [High Scores]
    The top 10 are saved in the file rogue.scr.
    Scores are entered automatically when you die, give up or win.
    The high score list is shown after the game ends.

  [Game Over Log]
    Details of your death (cause, final stats, recent messages) are
    written to the file gameover.log.

13. Hints and Strategy
================================================================================

  [Basic Strategy for Beginners]

  o Watch your food
    Hunger (Hungry -> Weak -> Faint) is one of the main causes of game over.
    Pick up any food (:) you find.
    If you have a ring of slow digestion, wear it.
    Be careful: some rings raise your food use (regeneration uses the most).

  o Fight carefully
    Being surrounded by several monsters gets dangerous fast.
    Lure monsters into a corridor (#) so they line up, and fight them one
    at a time.
    Running away when your HP is low is also important.
    HP recovers over time, but in a fight it may not recover fast enough.

  o Don't hold back with potions
    Use potions of healing and extra healing when you are in trouble.
    Try unknown potions when you have HP to spare.

  o Make good use of scrolls
    A scroll of magic mapping shows the layout of the floor and is very
    useful.
    Use scrolls of enchant weapon and enchant armor early to get stronger.
    A scroll of identify works on rings, wands, staffs, potions, weapons and
    armor.

  o Know your monsters
    - Leprechaun (L): it takes your gold, but your items are safe. Kill it
      and you get the gold back.
    - Nymph (N): it steals an item. Kill it to get the item back. You can
      also put important items on the floor first.
    - Ice Monster (I): appears from floors 2-11. A hit freezes you for
      2-3 turns, but does no direct HP damage. With AC 9 it is easy to hit.
      While you are paralyzed, monsters keep attacking you, so watch how
      many are around.
    - Aquator (A): it corrodes your armor. Protecting your armor with a
      scroll of protect armor is a good idea.
    - Wraith (W): 15% chance of draining a level. At 0 experience you die
      instantly. Attack from a distance or avoid it.
    - Venus Flytrap (F): once it grabs you, you take 1d6 damage every turn.
      Kill it with a wand of cancellation or by attacking from an adjacent
      square.
    - Phantom (P): an invisible monster on floors 15-24. Without see
      invisible it is shown only as "something". You can attack it, but it
      is hard to hit.
    - Dragon (D): if you are on a straight line with it, it breathes fire
      (6d6) from far away. Approach it off the rows, columns and diagonals.
      A wand of cancellation stops its fire.

  o Unidentified items
    Potions, scrolls, rings, wands and staffs are unknown when you pick
    them up.
    - Scrolls are fairly safe to try (some are bad, but few are deadly)
    - Try potions when your HP is full
    - Careful: a cursed ring can't be removed. A scroll of remove curse
      fixes this.
    - If you have a scroll of identify, using it first is safest.

  o Watch out for the Xeroc (X)
    A monster disguised as an item.
    You may be attacked when you try to pick up what looks like an item.

  o Use the search command "s"
    Press "s" to find hidden doors and traps on adjacent squares (without
    setting them off).
    Even what looks like a dead end may have a hidden door.
    With a ring of searching you search automatically every turn.

  o Bows and arrows
    If you throw arrows while wielding a short bow, the bow's bonuses are
    added.
    Arrows that hit a monster are destroyed, but arrows that miss fall to
    the floor and can be picked up.
    Darts and shuriken can be thrown as they are, without a bow.

  o Deep floors
    Beyond floor 26 (AMULETLEVEL), all monsters are scaled up.
    Once you have the Amulet, avoid fights as much as you can and head back
    quickly.
    On the way back, use the up stairs (the < key).
    Note that you can't go down stairs while levitating.
    Pressing < on floor 1 wins the game (you must have the Amulet).
